Facilitator Reference Only

RISE Score Interpretation Bands

Use these score ranges to interpret individual and cohort dimension results during facilitation:

Strong Growth 82–100%
Often responds by learning, adapting and moving forward.
Usually Adaptive 68–81%
Usually responds positively and adjusts when needed.
Depends on the Situation 52–67%
Response may change depending on the situation, level of risk or uncertainty.
More Cautious Under Pressure Below 52%
May prefer more certainty, time or support before moving forward.
Important Facilitator Principle: Use these bands as a reflection guide only. They are not fixed labels or psychometric classifications. Participant-facing views display only the behavioural category labels and RISE dimension results to avoid score comparison between participants. Keep discussions focused on situational tendencies rather than personality types.
